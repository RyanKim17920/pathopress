## Prov-GigaPath report-aligned limitation

The main article describes continued OpenCLIP training of both visual and text
encoders on 17,383 Providence WSI–report pairs and evaluates a held-out 20% of
patients. The zero-shot targets are NSCLC, COADREAD, LRP1B, KRAS, TP53, SPTA1,
FAT1, and KMT2D. These are private Providence evaluations, not downloadable
public benchmark leaves. Figure 4 and Extended Data Figure 9 show graphical bars
without exact numeric labels; the supplement and pinned official repository do
not provide their underlying values. Visual estimates are therefore excluded
rather than being represented as exact scores.
