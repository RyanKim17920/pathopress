Interpretation

Normalized scores place heterogeneous pathology endpoints on a common 0–100 direction-preserving scale. “Trust” means a retrospectively calibrated probability that absolute error is at most 10 points; it is not a clinical threshold or guarantee. Open a reported score’s source before citing it.
